Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

What this tool is

CD PPC OS is a single-operator tool used to access Google Ads and Merchant Center accounts that the operator manages or has been granted access to by the account owner.

What data it accesses

It reads campaign, keyword, search term, product, and performance data via the Google Ads API and the Merchant API. It does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any Google data outside of advertising and merchant accounts.

How that data is used

Data is used solely to produce reports, audits, and optimisation recommendations for the account owner it was retrieved from. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used to train models, or used for advertising or profiling.

Where data is stored

Retrieved data and OAuth credentials are stored locally on the operator's own machine. Credentials are never transmitted to any service other than Google. There is no hosted backend and no third-party analytics.

Revoking access

Any account owner may revoke this tool's access at any time via their Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
